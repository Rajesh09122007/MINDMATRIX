THE LOOP / RUN → THINK → LEVEL UP

Sprint through three-lane worlds, slide between lanes for data shards, then hit a question gate every few hundred metres. Your run pauses while you answer, so take a breath and think. Three gates clear a sector; the journey continues in a fresh place with a different subject. Shards earn 10 credits, correct answers earn 100 plus a streak bonus, and every third sector offers a spare heart. Misses cost a heart and reset your streak.

Optional browser-generated sound: a bright answer chime, a soft low note for a miss, and a short level-up flourish. Sound is off until you turn it on.
